## Notes

- E2 beats both baselines at day 14 and day 30; the P10–P90 band covers about 75–81% of actual balances (target about 80%).
- E3 lead time: across all alerted positives the median is 3 days, because many users are already short when the alert fires. For genuinely new shortfalls (balance still >= ৳200 at alert time) the median warning is 6 days, which meets the >= 5-day bar. Both numbers are reported.
- All results are on synthetic data and show that the pipeline works, not real-world accuracy (see docs/synthetic-data.md).
