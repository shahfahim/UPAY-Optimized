## 1. Executive Summary
Hishab AI is an intelligent, voice-enabled financial copilot designed specifically for low-income Mobile Financial Service (MFS) users in Bangladesh. Developed as a prototype for the upay ecosystem, it empowers marginalized demographics by providing AI-driven cash-flow forecasting, automated savings planning, and personalized financial education in native Bangla.

## 2. Problem Statement
Currently, MFS apps in Bangladesh serve merely as transactional tools (Send Money, Cash Out). Low-income users often face sudden liquidity shortages and lack access to proper financial planning. The barriers of complex UI and lack of financial literacy prevent them from managing their money effectively, leaving them vulnerable to financial shocks.

## 3. Proposed Solution
Team RageBait developed **Hishab AI**—a proactive financial health companion built directly into the MFS wallet. Instead of just showing past transactions, Hishab AI analyzes user behavior to forecast future cash flows, explain spending patterns in plain Bangla, and guide users toward realistic savings goals using a conversational AI interface.

## 4. Core Features
* **AI Cash-Flow Forecasting:** Utilizes machine learning to predict 30-day income and expense trends, alerting users of potential cash shortages before they happen.
* **Agent Locator (demo data):** Maps sample agents around the user. The cash hint is a fixed sample value, not a prediction; real agent-liquidity data from upay is required.
* **Inclusive Voice Assistant:** Features native Bangla Voice-to-Text and Text-to-Speech capabilities, ensuring maximum accessibility for users who cannot type or read complex terms.
* **Financial Goal Planner:** Turns user aspirations (e.g., saving for Eid, medical emergencies) into actionable, daily cash-flow-backed savings plans.

## 5. Responsible AI & Impact
Hishab AI strictly complies with Responsible AI principles. It provides explainable financial health signals but explicitly avoids making autonomous lending decisions. By increasing financial literacy and savings habits, Hishab AI drives user retention and ecosystem growth for upay while ensuring financial safety for its users.
